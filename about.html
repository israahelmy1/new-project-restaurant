Our story, since 2018

It started around a table

A place to slow down
and stay awhile.

In 2018, chef Elias Haddad and his sister Mira had a simple idea: bring people together over the food they grew up loving. The kind of food that takes its time, fills the kitchen with the smell of something wonderful, and never needs an occasion.

They found a little corner in Brooklyn, built a hearth, and named it after the two things that make their cooking sing: the golden warmth of saffron and the glow of an open ember.

Today, that same spirit is in every plate we send out and every guest we welcome. This is your neighborhood table, too.

With love, Mira & Elias

The hands behind the hearth

Meet Chef Elias.

For Elias, cooking has always been about memory. He grew up watching his grandmother feed a crowded table with a handful of fresh herbs and a fire that never quite went out.

After years in kitchens from Beirut to Brooklyn, he brought those flavors home — with a focus on beautiful produce, open-fire cooking, and food that makes you feel something.

His favorite part of the night? The first bite, just after the plates land.
